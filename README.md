# Revenue Engine

Mission: identify, validate, and execute legitimate opportunities capable of producing substantial revenue with minimal manual involvement.

## Operating rule
Ideas do not count. Research does not count. Repositories do not count. Only verified economics and movement toward payment count.

Every opportunity is scored on:
- speed to actual payment
- evidence of willing buyers
- likely profit after costs
- upfront cost / downside
- scalability
- automation leverage
- amount of Daniel involvement required

## Structure
- `data/opportunities.json` — live opportunity ledger
- `src/score.ts` — scoring engine
- `src/types.ts` — opportunity schema
- `docs/action-record.md` — evidence, costs, results, and next steps

The engine is intentionally category-agnostic. It can score software, bounties, reselling, licensing, brokerage-style opportunities, lead-gen assets, information products, arbitrage-like spreads that are legal and non-gambling, and other public-market opportunities.

## Truth standard
Revenue is only marked verified when there is evidence of cash received. Pipeline and potential stay separate.
